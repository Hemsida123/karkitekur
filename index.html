What we believe in

Architecture has a profound connection to its context, landscape and surroundings.

At the heart of our architectural work lies a commitment to transforming bold ideas into timeless work, where every detail is meticulously crafted.

We are recognised for our keen sensibility toward materials and detailing, as well as a true passion for craftsmanship in contemporary Nordic architecture and design.

01

Project L

Summerhouse · Stockholm archipelago 400 m² · Under construction

A summerhouse to be built on a beach plot in the Stockholm archipelago, offering magnificent sea views. The project is a souterrain house on a rocky cliff side, with the main spaces arranged around an inner courtyard overlooking the view.

The exterior is composed of a volume with walls of stacked thin limestone slabs on a masonry granite plinth, with on-site cast light concrete details and contrasting dark wooden windows and doors. The interior is done in dark woodwork against light lime-plaster textured walls.

Lead architect — Marko Koistinen Participating architect — Mattias Gavriél, Studio Gavriél Structural engineering — Astadien Byggkonsult
02

Villa Glanbäcken

Villa extension · Norrköping 170 m²

Grand Designs Sverige · TV4 · Autumn 2023

A larger villa extension to a small existing cabin on a high location overseeing Lake Glan in Norrköping. A simple monolithic volume with a dynamic inside, adding a double-height living room, a large kitchen and a top-floor master suite overlooking the lake.

The house is composed of a raw fir panelled facade, a raw pine panelled roof, zinc metal details and large wooden windows. The inside is designed with sober grey and natural tones, and light, delicate wooden details in oak, fir and pine.

Lead architect — Marko Koistinen Structural engineering — Astadien Byggkonsult
03

Project Koistinen

Summerhouse · Gnesta 85 m² · Built

A summerhouse built on a high location overseeing a lake in the heart of the Sörmland countryside. A simple black monolithic house with a double-height central living space organising the other rooms, and a two-sided symmetric exterior with large glazed sliding windows toward the lake.

The wooden construction is placed on five concrete walls cast on the bedrock. The exterior is black fir cladding, a black metal roof and black painted exposed beams; the interior is light wood, contrasting the dark outside.

Lead architect — Marko Koistinen
04

Fisherman's Cabin

Summerhouse · II, Finland 30 m² · Built

MTV3 · Spring 2022  ·  Avotakka / Meidän Mökki · 2021

A small summerhouse built on a remote island, on a beach plot on the Finnish shore of the northern Baltic. A simple, almost ascetic design with a single pitched roof toward the water and three symmetric double-opening glazed doors organising both the interior space and the main facade.

The exterior is raw fir panelled cladding, raw fir windows and a single pitched black metal roof. The driving challenges were a low budget, an off-grid location, spring flooding of the plot — and that every part of the project had to be drawn so it could be taken to the island by a small private boat during the short northern summer, or by snowmobile sled over the ice in winter.

Lead architect — Marko Koistinen

Who we are

Study model Project L

Marko Koistinen

Marko Koistinen Architects is an internationally active architecture and design studio led by architect Marko Koistinen, who brings Finnish heritage and training from the Royal Danish Academy of Fine Arts.

The studio focuses on contemporary experimental architecture and design, always prioritising high usability and property value for clients and partners.

We strongly believe that architecture has a profound connection to its context, landscape and environmental surroundings — and that the best solutions emerge through close, transparent collaboration with clients and partners, brought to life by skilled and talented craftsmen.

Our legacy is built on a tradition of innovation and craftsmanship, coupled with a relentless pursuit of perfection.
